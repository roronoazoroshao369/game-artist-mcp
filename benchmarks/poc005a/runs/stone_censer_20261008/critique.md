# Image-grounded critique — Stone Incense Burner (SELF_REVIEW)

The initial and revised preview PNGs were actually returned by the repository's live MCP server and inspected in this conversation.

## Initial image (revision 1)
The silhouette is recognizably a three-legged incense burner with a vented dome and a curling cyan smoke wisp. However, two evenly spaced dark round vents plus a central jade hexagon suggest a cartoon robot face. The stone lid and legs are too smooth/uniform, and the smoke is oversized, almost a bright blue ribbon.

## Targeted correction (revision 2)
Edited the SAME asset rather than recreating. Offset the lid vents and made them narrower, added a third angled vent, introduced irregular lid facets and chisel marks, split the lid rim, added cracks and sparse chipped stone details, and reduced the width/opacity of the smoke plume.

## Revised-image observation
The burner better reads as weathered carved stone. The anthropomorphic face impression is reduced but not entirely gone. Stone material and wear remain simple geometric abstractions with no physically nuanced texture; production visual quality is NOT established.
