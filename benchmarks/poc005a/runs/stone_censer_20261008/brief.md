# Held-out asset brief — Stone Incense Burner

Design an original heavy stone spirit-incense burner for an abandoned mountain cultivation shrine in a survival-action RPG. The stone censer is chipped, hand-carved, weathered and ancient rather than a shiny household lamp. It rests on three short legs, has a vented domed lid and releases one strange curling wisp of faint cyan spiritual smoke. A small jade inset or symbolic carving should identify its mystical function even in low light. Prioritize strong recognizable silhouette, irregular stone facets, credible wear and gameplay-size 64px/128px readability. Transparent single-prop artwork in dark-cultivation-v1, without text/logo or imitation of any existing game IP. Agent decides its own editable geometry via MCP; no prewritten art script or generative image model is allowed.
