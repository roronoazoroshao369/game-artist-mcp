# Image-grounded critique — Medicinal Pouch (SELF_REVIEW)

The initial and revised PNG outputs were returned by live stdio MCP render_preview and visually inspected.

## Initial image (revision 1)
The three green sprigs are recognizable, but the container reads like a rigid wooden barrel or jar instead of a flexible, worn fabric pouch. The front patch resembles a squared clipboard label. Lower fabric shows little sag/fold variation and the herb cluster is too evenly distributed.

## Targeted correction (revision 2)
Kept the same asset and changed body contour and fabric shadows into a softer bulging drawstring pouch; converted the front seal into an irregular stitched hanging patch; offset one herb leaf, added gathered pleats, drawstring loops, bottom stitches and a dried root. The result has more soft-cloth cues and less of the original barrel silhouette.

## Revised-image observation
Herb pouch is distinguishable from a jar at this size. The material contrast remains simplistic; folds are line-based and details may disappear at 64 px. No claim of production hand-painted quality.

## Limits
SELF_REVIEW only; no independent 64px/128px reviewer or host-origin provider attestation. The local JSON-RPC session shows actual render/edit behavior but never proves model authorship cryptographically.
