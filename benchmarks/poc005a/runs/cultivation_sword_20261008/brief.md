# Held-out asset brief — Cultivation Sword

Create one original **Tĩnh Lôi Kiếm** for a wandering cultivator in a dark, handmade survival-fantasy world. The sword has a long weathered forged blade, slightly irregular wear from decades of use, a dark antique bronze fitting and a jade spirit-energy inlay. It should feel functional and dangerous, not a shiny ceremonial toy. An aged red binding or seal fragment may suggest a talisman without becoming a text label.

Output a single isolated prop on a transparent canvas. Keep the sword readable in silhouette at 64 px and 128 px, use the project's dark-cultivation-v1 palette and coherent line weight. Do not copy existing game IP. No pre-authored geometry, coordinates or script is given with this brief. Do not use text-to-image, image-to-image or generative 3D tools.
